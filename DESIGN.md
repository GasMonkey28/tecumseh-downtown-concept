# Design research and rationale

This independent concept was revised on September 17, 2026. It borrows general information-design principles, not another organization's logo, copy, page template, imagery or code. Awards are evidence of recognition, not proof that every visitor prefers a design. The current reference sites may differ from their award-year versions.

## Reference points

| Reference | Verified recognition / relevance | Principle applied here |

| [Visit Ballarat](https://www.visitballarat.com.au/) | [2025 Australian Web Awards: Best in Show, User Experience](https://webawards.com.au/winners/2025/). ## Why this direction fits Tecumseh

The [official RFP](https://cms9files1.revize.com/cityoftecumseh/DDA/Documents/RFP%20-%20Downtown%20Tecumseh%20Website%202026.pdf) emphasizes destination experiences, prominent events, searchable businesses, historic identity, strong photography, Art Trail, Farmers Market, DDA information and mobile use. The proposed forest green, butter yellow, cream and restrained plum are a design direction, not approved City branding.

A real, credited archival street photograph gives the first screen a connection to Tecumseh. Original illustrated event posters and business artwork provide a consistent demonstration style. A production site should use current, approved photography of people, places, merchants and events; this archive image does not represent current storefronts or conditions.

## RFP-to-demo coverage

| RFP sections | Demonstrated | Still required for a complete project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4, 7, 14–15 | Photo-led homepage, featured sample events, clear entry points, desktop and phone layouts | Approved branding, current photography and content, further device/accessibility review |
| 8 | Month/list/calendar views, category/keyword/date filters, reset, detail dialog and shareable event URL | CMS authoring, recurring-event rules, real locations/maps, expiry workflow, full detail pages |
| 9 | Business search and categories, illustrated profiles and detail previews | Approved contact/hours/location fields, directory governance and staff editing |
| 10 | Dedicated Art Trail area, three selectable fictional stops, artwork summaries, official guide link | Approved sculptures/artists, actual route and map, photo rights, printable accessible guide |
| 11 | Dedicated market area, expandable FAQs, official schedule/vendor link | Approved season data, vendor profiles, announcements and content management |
| 12 | Clear links to official meeting records, streetscape update and business resources | Integrated, maintained DDA news, document library, staff and project content |
| 13, 16–24 | Static content structure only | CMS, migration, SEO setup, analytics, accessibility acceptance, hosting operations, training and ongoing services |

The interest-based day planner is our proposal, not an additional RFP requirement. It is deliberately small: no accounts, location tracking, bookings or personal-data collection. All event/business samples remain fictional, and the demo stays excluded from search indexing.

## Review route

1. Open the homepage, then a featured sample event.
2. Filter May 2027 to May 10–20; switch to calendar and try an empty range.
3. Select another Art Trail stop and expand the market FAQs.
4. Search for coffee and change the business category.
5. Switch the day planner to a creative afternoon and open the studio preview.
6. Follow an official DDA resource link, distinguishing real public information from sample records.
